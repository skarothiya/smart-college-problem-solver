Key Features

Built for Modern Campus Life

Designed with college students and administrators in mind for 100% transparency.

🤖

Intelligent Client Classifier

Our rule-based NLP algorithm analyzes problem descriptions on-the-fly and suggests the accurate category and urgency level instantly.

👍

Democratic Upvote Engine

Allow fellow students to upvote shared issues. High-upvote complaints gain top priority on the maintenance dashboard.

Admin Analytics Dashboard

Campus administrators get a real-time command center with breakdown stats (Pending, In Progress, Solved) and priority controls.
